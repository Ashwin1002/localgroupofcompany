About us

A local company, built for local growers and families.

Registered on 12 May 2024 as a private limited company, Local Group of Company Pvt. Ltd. operates from Fewa Marg in Ward No. 7 of Pokhara Metropolitan City, Kaski. We work across the agricultural chain — raising livestock and poultry, growing grains, legumes and oilseeds, supporting farmers with crop and animal production services, and preparing produce for market.

Through Local Bazar, our retail and community presence, we bring that produce and know-how closer to people — so families in Pokhara can buy local, and farmers can sell closer to home.

नेपाली मान्छे, नेपाली उत्पादन

Our motto: “Nepali people, Nepali products.” We believe Nepal's own farmers can feed Nepal's own families, and every purchase from a local producer keeps value in our communities.

Our values

What we do

Field, farm and market — under one roof.

Our registered business activities cover the full journey from field and farm to the market shelf.

Specialised Retail

Retail sale of selected goods through specialised stores, bringing local products closer to customers.

Grains, Pulses & Oilseeds

Cultivation of food grains (other than paddy), legumes and oilseed crops.

Cattle & Buffalo Farming

Raising healthy cattle and buffaloes under veterinary-guided care.

Poultry & Birds

Raising ducks, chickens, pigeons and turkeys — the core of our MD's expertise.

Crop Production Support

Support activities that help farmers grow better crops, from field preparation to good cultivation practice.

Livestock Production Support

Support services for animal production, backed by formal training in veterinary science and livestock management.

Post-Harvest Handling

Preparing harvested crops for market: cleaning, trimming and grading.
